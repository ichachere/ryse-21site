About Ryse 21.

Who we are

A holding company built to buy, hold, and operate.

Ryse 21 is a holding company that acquires and operates small, cash-flowing businesses for the long term. We buy companies we can run remotely and intend to hold, staying close to the work after we close.

We look for operations with a purchase price under $2 million, fewer than five employees, at least 24 months of consistent cash flow, and a model that can be managed off-site. Our portfolio includes businesses in equipment rental, telemetry, industrial repair services, vending and equipment leasing, retail research chemicals, and treasury management.
