Who we are

A holding company built to hold and operate.

Ryse 21 is a holding company that owns and operates small businesses. We're always open to opportunities we can run remotely and hold for the long term.

When an opportunity makes sense, we look for a purchase price under $2 million, fewer than five employees, at least 24 months of consistent cash flow, and a model that can be managed off-site. Our portfolio includes businesses in equipment rental, telemetry, industrial repair services, vending and equipment leasing, retail research chemicals, and treasury management.
