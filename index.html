About

Built to buy, hold, and run cash-flowing businesses.

Ryse 21 buys and manages cash-flowing small businesses. We look for operations we can run remotely and hold for the long term — staying close to the work after we buy.

Ryse 21 owns and manages a portfolio of businesses spanning equipment rental, telemetry, industrial repair services, retail research chemicals, and a personal treasury management system.

Acquisitions

What we look for.

Ryse 21 acquires cash-flowing small businesses we can manage off-site. We typically look for:

  • Purchase price under $2 million

    We focus on acquisitions with a purchase price under $2 million.

  • One to three employees

    Small teams — typically one to three employees.

  • Consistent cash flow

    A history of consistent cash flow, not a turnaround or a startup idea.

  • Managed remotely or off-site

    The business can be managed remotely or off-site. The owner is not required to stay on site.
